# Kisan Mitra: demo video script (2 min 30 s)

**Required by the form:** a 2–3 minute demo of the build. Aim for **2:30**.
**Recording:** phone screen recorder (best), or laptop with **Win + Alt + R** and the browser window narrowed to phone size.
Speak the voiceover live while recording, or add it afterwards (Clipchamp is built into Windows).
**Before you start:** quiet room, no headphones (so the app's voice is recorded), app open on the language screen, a real leaf ready.

---

## Part 1: problem (0:00–0:20)
**Screen:** you on camera, or the app icon / home screen.
**Say:**
> "Hi, I'm Shiva from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru. India has about a hundred and forty-six million farm families.
> When a crop gets sick or a farmer needs scheme information, expert help is far away, and most farmers would rather
> *speak* in their own language than type in English. So I built **Kisan Mitra**, a voice assistant for farmers,
> powered by Gnani AI's voice models."

## Part 2: built for every farmer (0:20–0:35)
**Screen:** the language picker. Tap **हिन्दी**. The app speaks the greeting.
**Say:**
> "You choose your language by its script, not by reading English. There are ten Indian languages plus Hinglish, and the
> app talks back to confirm. Every button is then in your own language, with one big button to talk."

## Part 3: live voice conversation in Hindi (0:35–1:05)
**Screen:** tap the big green **बात करें** button. Let the greeting play, then ask:
> **"मेरे टमाटर के पत्ते पीले होकर मुड़ रहे हैं, क्या करूँ?"**
> *(My tomato leaves are turning yellow and curling. What should I do?)*

Let the full answer play (captions visible). Then point at the speed line and **say:**
> "Gnani's **Prisma** model transcribed that in about a hundred milliseconds, and Gnani's **Timbre** speaks the answer
> sentence by sentence, so the farmer hears a reply in about two seconds."

## Part 4: weather-aware advice (1:05–1:25)
**Screen:** same call. Ask:
> **"इस हफ्ते बारिश होगी क्या? दवा कब छिड़कूँ?"**
> *(Will it rain this week? When should I spray?)*

**Say (after the answer):**
> "It uses the live weather forecast for the farmer's location, so it can say *don't spray before the rain*."

## Part 5: another language (1:25–1:50)
**Screen:** end the call (red button), tap the language button, and pick **ಕನ್ನಡ** (or **తెలుగు**). Show the screen switching script.
Tap **ಮಾತನಾಡಿ** and ask:
> **"ಭತ್ತಕ್ಕೆ ಯಾವ ಗೊಬ್ಬರ ಹಾಕಬೇಕು?"** *(Which fertiliser should I use for paddy?)*
> Telugu alternative: **"వరికి ఏ ఎరువు వేయాలి?"**

**Say:**
> "The same in Kannada: Prisma detects the language, and Timbre answers in a native Kannada voice."

## Part 6: crop photo diagnosis (1:50–2:10)
**Screen:** end the call. Tap **📷 crop photo**, take a photo of the leaf, and the answer appears and is spoken.
**Say:**
> "Farmers can also just take a photo of a sick crop and hear what's wrong and what to do, in their language."

## Part 7: how it works and what's next (2:10–2:30)
**Screen:** settings ⚙️ → **System check** (all the Gnani checks green), then the end card.
**Say:**
> "Under the hood: the phone streams audio to Gnani Prisma's real-time speech-to-text, a reasoning model answers using
> live weather and scheme facts, and Gnani Timbre streams the voice back. It also works on eight-kilohertz phone audio,
> so the next step is a toll-free number for farmers without smartphones. Kisan Mitra: an expert in every farmer's pocket,
> in their own language. Thank you, Gnani AI."

**End card (last 3 s):** `Kisan Mitra · Voice by Gnani Prisma + Timbre · #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge #GnaniAI`

---

## Recording tips
1. Do **one practice run**, and check that every answer plays.
2. After each question, **stay silent** until it answers (it waits for about half a second of silence).
3. If one take fails or is slow, cut it in editing. Keep the final video **between 2:00 and 3:00**.
4. Upload it to **YouTube (Unlisted)** or **Google Drive (Anyone with the link can view)**, and use that link in the form.

## Don't show (unreliable right now)
- **Mandi prices.** The government API is down.
- **Latest scheme dates.** Live search needs the paid Gemini tier.
- **Interrupting the bot mid-answer.** It needs headphones.
